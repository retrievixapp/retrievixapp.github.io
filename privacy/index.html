Retrievix

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026
In short: Retrievix finds hidden photos, videos, audio and documents that are still stored on your device and helps you bring them back. Everything happens on your phone: your files, file names, scan results and backed-up messages are never uploaded. The app has no ads, no analytics and no account, and it does not connect to the internet.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Retrievix Android app (“Retrievix”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name com.retrievix.recovery. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

  • Scan. Retrievix looks through the folders you allow (see section 3) for photos, videos, audio and documents that you normally can’t see: files in hidden folders, files the system has moved to its trash, cached copies, and files whose name or extension doesn’t match their content. To do this it reads file names, sizes, dates, folder structure and the beginning of each file (to recognise its type). This happens only on your device.
  • Recover. When you recover a file, the app moves it into the app’s private Recycle Bin: it copies the file, checks that the copy is complete, and then deletes the original. From the Recycle Bin you can export files to Pictures/Retrievix, Movies/Retrievix, Music/Retrievix or Documents/Retrievix, or delete them.
  • Vault. Files you import into the Vault are copied into the app’s private storage and the originals are deleted. You can restore them to the public folders above at any time. The Vault is not encrypted and has no password.
  • Search. Searching for documents by name covers the folders you allow and is done locally on your device.
  • WhatsApp message backup (optional). If you turn this feature on and grant notification access, the app saves the text of WhatsApp message notifications your phone receives — conversation name, sender name, message text and time — in a database on your device, so you can still read them later. It does not read notifications from other apps, does not upload anything, and you can delete saved conversations in the app. To stop it, turn off notification access for Retrievix in your system settings.
  • Uninstalling deletes app data. Files in the Recycle Bin and the Vault, and saved messages, are stored inside the app. Export or restore anything you want to keep before uninstalling.
  • No guarantee of recovery. Files that have been fully erased from storage cannot be read without system-level (root) access, which the app does not use.

4. No advertising, analytics or tracking

Retrievix does not show ads and does not include any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. It does not use your advertising ID, and because it has no internet access, nothing about you, your files or your messages is sent from the app to us or to anyone else.

5. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, files or messages through the app.

If you contact us by email (for example via “Feedback” in the app), we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.
